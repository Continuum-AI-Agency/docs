Your brand book is the single source of truth for how your brand looks, sounds, and shows up. Continuum drafts it from your website during onboarding and deepens it over time. It isn't just reference — it actively **grounds** [Canvas](/canvas/skills-and-grounding) generations and steers the [agents](/concepts), so your output stays on-brand by default.

## Where it lives

Open **Settings → Brand Intelligence**. Four scores sit at the top — identity readiness, evidence coverage, competitor coverage, and observed visibility — above six tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The evidence behind your brand profile, and when it was last refreshed. |
| **Brand DNA** | Identity (color palette, typography, voice, personality, imagery, and content rules), strategy (positioning and audience), and the editable `brand.md`. |
| **Competition** | The competitors Continuum tracks for you, with their status and a strategic summary. |
| **Visibility** | A simulated estimate of how visible your brand is in AI answers. It's an estimate, not observed ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI data. |
| **Readiness** | Your [brand readiness score](/brand/readiness) and what would raise it. |
| **Sources** | The knowledge-base documents and onboarding intake behind the book. |

Below the tabs, the **design system** section holds the visual sources Continuum applies to creative.

## Brand kit

The brand kit is the deterministic core of your identity — the pieces any feature can pull by brand:

- **Logo** — your logo file.
- **Colors** — your brand palette as hex values.
- **Typography** — your brand typefaces, stored as font-family names.

<Info>Typography is stored as font names, not font files — Continuum references the families. (Client After Effects projects are the exception: those require the actual font files. See [After Effects delivery](/forge/after-effects/delivery).)</Info>

## Generating and editing

If your brand book is empty, select **Generate Brand Book** to build it from your website and onboarding answers. Edit the **Authoritative brand document** — the `brand.md` under **Brand DNA** — to change what agents must follow. Derived intelligence never overwrites your edits.

## Working across brands

Each brand has its own brand book, kit, and connected accounts. Switch the **active brand** from the brand switcher to move between them, or create a new brand profile. A brand that hasn't finished onboarding or has no active integration is flagged so you know what to finish.
