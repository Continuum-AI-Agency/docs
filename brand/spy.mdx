Brand Spy is your competitor intelligence workspace. It tracks the brands you compete with — their organic Instagram posts and their paid ad creatives — so you can see what they're running and pull ideas into your own work.

<Note>Brand Spy is in the **Creative+** section of the sidebar.</Note>

## The four tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Inspiration** | A browser of competitor organic posts and paid ad creatives; save anything to a board. |
| **Report** | An awareness report on how you stack up against tracked competitors. |
| **Boards** | Your saved inspiration boards. |
| **Competitors** | The competitors you track, each with a health signal, plus recommended ones to add. |

## Tracking competitors

<Steps>
<Step title="Add competitors">
Search for a competitor and add them to your tracked list. Continuum also recommends competitors based on your onboarding.
</Step>

<Step title="Browse their creative">
In **Inspiration**, switch between organic posts and paid ad creatives, and filter by competitor.
</Step>

<Step title="Save to a board">
Save the creatives worth referencing to a board so your team can find them later.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Tracking a competitor's organic posts uses Instagram data — connect an Instagram account in [Integrations](/integrations) to get the most out of Brand Spy.</Tip>
