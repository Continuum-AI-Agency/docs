The Library is your brand's media home. Every creative — generated, uploaded, or pulled from a campaign — lives here with its versions, review status, and comments, and you can share it with people outside your account.

## Media and Inspiration

The Library has two tabs:

- **Media** — your brand's own images and video.
- **Inspiration** — competitor organic posts and paid ad creatives to draw from. This is the same engine as [Brand Spy](/brand/spy).

## Find your way around

The sidebar groups media so you can jump straight to it:

| Group | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Home**, **Everything** | A starting view, and every asset. |
| **Images**, **Videos**, **Typography** | Media by type. |
| **Canvas** | Outputs from the [canvas](/canvas/overview). |
| **Elements** | Your saved [Elements](/canvas/elements). |
| **Source files**, **Templates** | Design files and templates. |
| **Needs review** | Work waiting on a decision. |
| **Pipelines** | Published [pipelines](/canvas/pipelines), ready to run. |
| **Saved views**, **Collections**, **Trash** | Your own groupings, and deleted items. |

Switch between **grid**, **list**, **board**, and **reel** layouts.

## Where media comes from

Every asset carries its source, so you can filter by where it came from: **Uploads**, **AI Creations**, **Canvas**, **Inspiration**, **Ad Creatives**, **HyperFrames**, **Chat Uploads**, **Clips**, **Reels**, **Imported**, **Figma**, and **Forge renders**.

## Review and approve

Every asset has a review status: **None**, **Draft**, **In review**, **Needs changes**, or **Approved**. Your brand can rename these to match how your team works. Choose **Request review** to ask a teammate to look.

**Comments** can be pinned to a spot on an image or a moment in a video. They support `@`mentions and attachments, and you can lock a comment so it's internal only.

## Share outside your account

Create a **share link** to show work to a client or partner. Choose whether people can download, comment, or approve, and add a passcode and your logo.

## Use your media

- **Drag it onto the [canvas](/canvas/overview)** as a reference.
- **Edit video** in [Video Studio](/canvas/video-studio).
- **Run a pipeline** with an asset as input.
- **Put finished creative on a post** in the [content calendar](/organic/calendar).

## From your AI assistant

Find anything by describing it, see where a creative has run, comment, and set review status from Claude or ChatGPT. Editing or deleting comments, collections, and share links stays in the app. See [Make creative](/mcp/use-cases/creative).
