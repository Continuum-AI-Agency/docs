The **Action** section of the **Add Node** menu holds two kinds of node. **Action** nodes apply one deterministic operation, with no generation and the same result every time. **Tools** shape how your graph runs and what comes out of it.

## Action

One operation, picked from the **Image ›**, **Video ›**, or **Text ›** menus.

### Image

| Group | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| **Colour** | Colour Grade, Filter, Tint, Blur, Shader Effect |
| **Transform** | Rotate, Flip, Chroma Key, Remove Background, Crop to Ratio, Pad to Ratio, Duplicate |
| **Overlay** | Burn In Text, Call To Action, Burn In Image |

### Video

Speed, Ken Burns, Stitch, Audio Bed, Split, Reverse, Boomerang, Long Exposure, Watermark, Greenscreen, Burn In, Subtitles, Editorial Captions, Extract Frames, and Frame Grid, among others.

### Text

Split Text, Find & Replace, and Join Text.

## Tools

| Node | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Batch** | A list of up to 100 inputs. Everything downstream runs once per item. Combine lists item by item (**zip**) or every item with every other (**cross**). |
| **Router** | Sends one output to several places without running it twice. |
| **Export** | Saves up to 20 media out as PNG, JPG, or WEBP; MP4, MOV, or GIF; or a ZIP of a batch. |
| **Continuity Frame** | Extracts the first, last, or an exact frame of a video as an image. |
