Delivery nodes close the loop from canvas to channel. They live under **Action › Implementation** in the **Add Node** menu.

## Planner Draft

Finds or creates an organic draft in the [Calendar](/organic/calendar), with its caption, schedule, and creative.

**Inputs → outputs:** a caption plus images or video → a Planner draft.

## Post to Platform

Posts a saved Planner draft. Connect a **Planner Draft** node to it, then choose:

- **Post now** — publish straight away.
- **Schedule for its planner time** — publish at the time set in the Calendar.

See [Publishing](/organic/publishing) for where you can post.

**Inputs → outputs:** a Planner draft → a post.

## Paid Ad

Replaces the creative on a paused or active Meta ad.

**Inputs → outputs:** an image, carousel, or video → the ad's new creative.

<Note>Older canvases that used the **Video Splicer** or **Publish to Planner** nodes are converted automatically when they load: Video Splicer becomes a **Video Editor** node, and Publish to Planner becomes the publish node.</Note>

## Try it

Ask Claude or ChatGPT:

- "Put the finished image on next Tuesday's post."
- "Swap the winning image into our spring sale ad. Show me first."
- "Undo the creative swap on that ad."

Swapping a live ad's creative shows a preview and waits for your yes.
