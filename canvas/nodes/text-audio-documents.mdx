These nodes feed words, sound, and knowledge into the rest of your graph. They live in the **Text**, **Audio**, and **Document** sections of the **Add Node** menu.

## Text Block

A prompt or enrichment input. Wire it into a generator's prompt.

**Inputs → outputs:** optional image, audio, document, or video → text.

## Note / Annotation

A free-text note on the canvas, for comments and instructions to your team. Press `⌘B` for bold. A note never runs and isn't part of the graph.

## Audio Reference

Brings in voice or sound.

**Inputs → outputs:** none → audio.

## Document Context

Adds PDF or text knowledge to a generation.

**Inputs → outputs:** none → document.

## Try it

Bring a product spec into a generation:

1. Add a **Document Context** node from the **Document** section, and load your product one-pager PDF.
2. Connect it to a **Text Block** that describes the shot, such as "hero shot of the bottle with the three key benefits".
3. Connect the Text Block to an **Image Generation** node's prompt, and choose **Run Node**.

Add a **Note / Annotation** beside the graph, such as "Use approved product shots only", so teammates know the rules.
