A **pipeline** is a saved canvas published with declared inputs and outputs — a recipe for a type of creative, such as a product lifestyle shot. Anyone on the brand can run it with new material, without opening the graph.

## Publish a pipeline

<Steps>
<Step title="Leave the inputs open">
Build the graph, and leave at least one required input unwired — a reference image or a prompt. Those open ports become the pipeline's inputs.
</Step>
<Step title="Switch to Pipeline">
Right-click the canvas, choose **Save**, and switch the toggle to **Pipeline**. The dialog shows what it **Takes** as inputs.
</Step>
<Step title="Describe it">
Fill in **What it makes and when to use it**.
</Step>
<Step title="Write the agent guide">
Write or draft the **Agent guide** — when to use it and when to avoid it. Agents read it to decide when to run the pipeline.
</Step>
<Step title="Publish">
Choose **Publish pipeline**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Run a pipeline

Open **Pipelines** in the [Library](/canvas/library) sidebar, pick one, and choose **Set up run**. Provide the inputs — text, a Library asset, or an [Element](/canvas/elements) — and choose **Run pipeline**. The run shows its outputs and cost when it finishes.

Published pipelines also appear in the **Pipelines** tab of **My Workflows** on the canvas.

## From your AI assistant

Ask Claude or ChatGPT to run one — "Run our customer-testimonial pipeline with these three angles: taste, price, convenience." From your assistant, one request can make up to 10 versions. See [Make creative](/mcp/use-cases/creative).
