Two things keep canvas output on-brand without extra prompting. **Brand grounding** steers each generation with your brand book, creative direction, and design system. **Creative skills** add reusable creative direction on top.

## The grounding chip

Every generation node carries a grounding chip, such as **Brand · Light · Skills 2**. Open it to see and change what steers that node. It has four sections:

| Section | What it applies |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand book** | Pieces of your [brand book](/brand/overview). |
| **Creative direction** | How your brand looks and behaves in creative. |
| **Design system** | Sections of your brand's design system. |
| **Creative skills** | The skills you've added to this node. |

## How much of the brand book applies

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Full** | Applies the entire brand book. |
| **Light** | Applies colors, typography, and logo. |
| **Off** | Applies none of it. |
| **N pieces** | Applies only the pieces you pick. |

Nodes you add by hand start on **Light**. Nodes the Composer builds use the full brand book.

The brand-book pieces are **Colors**, **Typography**, **Voice**, **Vision / Imagery**, **Personality**, **Audience**, and **Logo**, or the **Entire brand book**.

<Tip>To ground several nodes at once, select them, right-click, and choose **Enforce brand book on selection**.</Tip>

## Creative direction

Creative direction captures how your brand behaves in creative, beyond the basics. Pieces include **Visual thesis**, **Composition**, **Typography behaviour**, **Colour behaviour**, **Photography**, **Illustration and graphics**, **Motion**, **People and characters**, **The product in the world**, **Where the mark goes**, **Brand signature**, and **Refusals**.

## Design system

Apply sections of your brand's design system: **Palette**, **Typography**, **Spacing**, **Radii**, **Shadows**, **Layout**, **Components**, **Iconography**, **Motion**, **Voice**, **Imagery**, **Logo**, and **Formats**. To bring one section in as a visible reference, use a [Design Reference node](/canvas/nodes/image).

## Shortcuts in the Composer

| Shortcut | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `/brand` | Ground in your brand. |
| `/brand:colors` | Ground in your colors. |
| `/brand:logo` | Ground in your logo. |
| `/nobrand` | Turn brand grounding off. |

## Creative skills

A **creative skill** is a reusable creative-direction directive — a repeatable instruction for how something should look or feel. Skills are folded into canvas generations and agent content behind the scenes.

- Add skills to a node from the **Creative skills** section of its grounding chip, or type `@` in the Composer.
- Manage skills in **Settings → Skills**. Create your own, browse the first-party library, and edit or archive existing ones.

Grounding steers a generation. It doesn't block anything. Pieces your brand book hasn't built yet can't be applied — a nudge to [deepen your brand book](/brand/readiness).
