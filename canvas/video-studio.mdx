**Video Studio** is Continuum's video editor. Turn raw footage into cuts for social — trim pauses, add captions and music, cut to the beat, and reformat for Reels, TikTok, or Shorts.

## Open Video Studio

- From the [Library](/canvas/library): select a video and choose **Edit video** or **Edit in timeline**, or start a **New edit**.
- From the canvas: open a [Video Editor node](/canvas/nodes/video).

## The workspace

| Area | Tabs |
| --- | --- |
| **Left panel** | **Media**, **Text**, **Graph**, **Generate** |
| **Right panel** | **Inspector**, **Agent** |

## Quick edits

| Edit | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Cut pauses** | Removes silences. |
| **Auto-captions** | Adds captions from the speech. |
| **Detect beats** | Finds the beats in the music. |
| **Cut on beat** | Cuts the edit to the beat. |

Music beds are available too.

## Editing

The timeline supports **Split at playhead**, trimming the start or end to the playhead, **Ripple delete**, copy, paste, and duplicate, markers, and undo and redo. You can also:

- **First cut from a brief…** — describe the edit and get a first cut, with variants.
- **Ask the agent** — make changes in plain language.
- **Generate media** — create new footage or stills for the edit.

## Formats

Reformat for **TikTok**, **Instagram Reels**, **YouTube Shorts**, **YouTube**, **Feed 1:1**, or **Feed 4:5**.

## Export

Choose **Export…** to render an H.264 MP4. It's saved to your Library, with **Download MP4** and **Open in Library** when it's done.

## From your AI assistant

Ask Claude or ChatGPT to edit — "Cut the pauses from my latest video and add captions." Every change shows up live in Video Studio, and every edit is saved as a version you can undo. See [Edit video](/mcp/use-cases/video).
