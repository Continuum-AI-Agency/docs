Once a graph works, you don't want to rebuild it. Save it as a **workflow** to reload and rerun it, or save part of it as a **technique** to drop into other graphs.

## Save a workflow

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the save dialog">
Right-click the canvas and choose **Save** from **Canvas Actions**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose Workflow">
The dialog has a **Workflow** | **Pipeline** toggle. Leave it on **Workflow** to save the graph for yourself and your team. Choose **Pipeline** to publish it with declared inputs; see [Pipelines](/canvas/pipelines).
</Step>
<Step title="Name it and save">
Give it a name you'll recognize later.
</Step>
</Steps>

Your canvas also autosaves as you go, so you won't lose a graph in progress. A saved workflow is what you reload on purpose.

## Load a workflow

Choose **Load Workflow** from **Canvas Actions** to open **My Workflows**. It has three tabs:

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Pre-mades** | Templates that ship with Continuum. A good place to start. |
| **Saved** | Workflows your team has saved. |
| **Pipelines** | Published pipelines. |

From **My Workflows** you can search, load, rename, and delete.

## Rerun with new inputs

Load a workflow, swap the inputs — a new product shot, a different prompt — and choose **Run Flow**. Nodes that didn't change are skipped. Choose **Rerun all** to regenerate everything.

## Techniques

A **technique** is a saved selection of nodes. Select the nodes, right-click, and choose **Save selection**. Techniques appear in the **Techniques** submenu at the top of **Add Node**, so you can drop a proven chunk of graph into any canvas.
