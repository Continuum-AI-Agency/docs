Notable product and documentation updates, newest first.

<Update label="November 2026" description="Documentation">
  The [tool reference](/mcp/tools) now covers every tool the connector lists, plus the ones it accepts by name. Each area page gives each tool's role, confirmation, inputs, an example call, and its errors, and agents can read it all as [JSON](/mcp/tools.json). New: Forge [Figma import](/forge/functions#forge_manage-source_figma), the per-action [confirmation lifetimes](/mcp/reference#confirmations), and how an Admin or Owner [approves a change](/mcp/control#what-waits-for-your-yes) from an assistant.
</Update>

<Update label="October 2026" description="Rates">
  New [Providers and rates](/providers-and-rates) page: who makes each model and what each generation costs in Canvas credits. Generated voiceovers and music now use Canvas credits too. Google retired Veo 3.1, so Veo requests now run on Omni 1.1 Flash at Omni's rate.
</Update>

<Update label="October 2026" description="Documentation">
  Rebuilt the docs around using Continuum from your AI assistant. New guides cover [connecting Claude, ChatGPT, and Claude Code](/quickstart#connect-your-assistant), [what you can do](/mcp/overview), [prompt recipes](/mcp/recipes), [what waits for your yes](/mcp/control), and a [tool reference](/mcp/tools). The product guides are now organized by product line: [Creative+](/canvas/overview), [Organic+](/organic/overview), and [Performance+](/performance/overview). The site has a new look to match trycontinuum.ai.
</Update>

<Update label="July 2026" description="Documentation">
  Rebuilt the documentation to match the shipped product — new guides for the [AI Canvas](/canvas/overview), [Organic+](/organic/overview), [Performance+](/performance/overview), [Brand](/brand/overview), and [Brand Spy](/brand/spy).
</Update>
