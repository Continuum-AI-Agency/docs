Continuum brings planning, creative production, publishing, and optimization into one place, all scoped to a brand. A few concepts explain how it fits together.

## Everything is scoped to a brand

A **brand** (or brand profile) is the unit everything hangs off: its [brand book](/brand/overview), connected accounts, media, drafts, and analytics. If you manage more than one brand, you switch the **active brand** from the brand switcher, and the whole app re-scopes to it. In your AI assistant, you say "Switch to Acme Coffee." Each brand keeps its own identity, integrations, and history.

## The brand book grounds everything

Your **brand book** is a living, AI-generated identity — colors, typography, voice, audience, positioning, and guidelines — first drafted from your website and deepened over time. It actively **grounds** generations on the Canvas and steers the agents, so output stays on-brand by default. A **brand readiness score** tracks how complete your brand book is; it's informational and blocks nothing.

## Two agents do the analysis

- **Organic agent** — your social strategist. It plans posts, drafts concepts, generates plans in bulk, searches your media, and hands work into the calendar.
- **Jaina** — your ads analyst. Ask about performance and Jaina returns a structured report: KPIs, insights, and prioritized recommendations.

The **Optimizer** sits beside Jaina in Performance+. The Optimizer proposes budget moves, Jaina explains, and you approve.

## Use it from your AI assistant

Everything above is also reachable from Claude, ChatGPT, or any assistant that supports custom connectors. You connect once with the [Model Context Protocol (MCP)](/mcp/overview), sign in with your Continuum login, and ask in your own words. Your role and brand access carry over, and anything that publishes, changes your ads, or deletes something waits for your yes.

## The creative loop

These pieces form a loop you repeat:

<Steps>

<Step title="Create">
Generate creative on the [AI Canvas](/canvas/overview) and plan posts on the [calendar](/organic/calendar).

<Step title="Publish">

</Step>
</Steps>

## Key terms

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand / brand profile** | The scope for all work; you can manage several and switch the active one. |
| **Brand book** | The AI-generated brand identity that grounds generations and agents. |
| **Brand readiness score** | A 0–100 measure of how complete your brand book is. Informational only. |
| **AI Canvas** | The node-based surface where you generate and edit creative. Labeled **Canvas** in the app. |
| **Node / workflow** | A single block on the canvas; a saved graph of connected nodes. |
| **Creative** | Produced content (an image, video, or ad). An **asset** is a raw file. |
| **Draft** | A planned organic post in the calendar. |
| **Connector** | The link that lets Claude, ChatGPT, or another assistant work in Continuum for you. |

See the [glossary](/glossary) for the full list.
