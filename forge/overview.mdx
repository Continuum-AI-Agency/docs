**Forge** turns a design file into a template you can render again and again. Fill the template's fields with your messages, products, and offers, preview every version, and render the set into your Library or onto a Meta ad. Your design files in, on-brand versions out.

<Info>Forge is in **Creative+** in the sidebar and is part of the Performance+ plan. See [Billing](/settings/billing).</Info>

## The three tabs

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Templates** | Your templates. Filter by **All**, **Ready**, **Drafts**, **Needs attention**, or **Shared with you**, and by animated or static. |
| **Render** | Fill a template's fields row by row, preview each version, and render. |
| **Render ledger** | Everything you've rendered, and how many renders are waiting for approval. |

## Bring in a design file

Drop a project into Forge. It accepts After Effects files (`.aep`, `.aepx`, `.aet`), a `.zip` of a packaged project, Photoshop (`.psd`) and Illustrator (`.ai`) files, and fonts (`.ttf`, `.otf`).

<Tip>For After Effects projects, follow [After Effects delivery](/forge/after-effects/overview) to structure the project so every field you want to change is exposed cleanly.</Tip>

## Render versions

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick a template">
Open a template that's **Ready**, then go to **Render**.
</Step>
<Step title="Fill the rows">
Each row is one version. Type the values in, or choose **Draft rows with AI** to write them from a brief.
</Step>
<Step title="Preview">
Each row shows a live composed preview, so you see every version before anything is rendered.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose where it goes">
Render to the **Library only**, or **Replace an ad** to put the version on an active or paused Meta ad.
</Step>
<Step title="Render">
Final renders land in your Library under **Forge renders**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Render sets

Save a group of rows as a **render set** to come back to it. You can rename, duplicate, download, and share a set, see its version history, and update it to the template's current version.

## Approvals and Meta delivery

Renders headed for a Meta ad go through approval. Approvals can come from Continuum, Slack, or WhatsApp.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting on you** | The render needs your approval. |
| **Publishing…** | The approved version is being placed on the ad. |
| **Published, paused** | The version is on the ad, and the ad is paused. |
| **Approved — Meta writes are off** | Approved, but this brand doesn't allow Forge to write to Meta. |
| **Rejected**, **Publishing failed**, **Expired** | Nothing was placed. |

Only an Owner or Admin can turn Meta writes on or off for a brand.

## From your AI assistant

Ask Claude or ChatGPT to fill a template from a brief — "Write six versions of the promo template from this brief." You see a preview of every version, and nothing is made until you confirm. Final versions need an Admin or Owner. See [Produce templates at scale](/mcp/use-cases/templates).
