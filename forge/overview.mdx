**Forge** fills your design templates — ready-made layouts, such as a promo video or a story card — to make many on-brand versions in one go. Fill a template's fields with your messages, products, and offers, preview every version, and render the set into your Library.

<Info>Forge is in **Creative+** in the sidebar and is part of the Performance+ plan. See [Billing](/settings/billing).</Info>

## The three tabs

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Templates** | Your templates. Filter by **All**, **Ready**, **Drafts**, **Needs attention**, or **Shared with you**, and by animated or static. |
| **Render** | Fill a template's fields row by row, preview each version, and render. |
| **Render ledger** | Everything you've rendered. |

## Get a template set up

Templates are built from your team's design files. For an After Effects project, follow [After Effects delivery](/forge/after-effects/overview) to structure it so every field you want to change is exposed cleanly, then hand it to your Continuum contact.

## Render versions

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick a template">
Open a template that's **Ready**, then go to **Render**.
</Step>
<Step title="Fill the rows">
Each row is one version. Type the values in, or choose **Draft rows with AI** to write them from a brief.
</Step>
<Step title="Preview">
Each row shows a live composed preview, so you see every version before anything is rendered.
</Step>
<Step title="Render">
Final renders land in your Library under **Forge renders**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Render sets

Save a group of rows as a **render set** to come back to it. You can rename, duplicate, download, and share a set, see its version history, and update it to the template's current version.

## Try it

In the app, open a **Ready** template, go to **Render**, choose **Draft rows with AI**, and give it a brief such as "Six versions of our spring offer: three for price, three for free shipping."

Or ask Claude or ChatGPT:

- "Which templates do we have?"
- "Write six versions of the promo template from this brief."
- "Produce the whole set. Show me what will be made first."

From your assistant, you see a preview of every version, and nothing is made until you confirm. Filling a template needs the **Operator** role or higher, and final versions, rather than draft previews, need an **Admin** or **Owner**. See [Produce templates at scale](/mcp/use-cases/templates).

For agent and application integration, use the [Forge agent quickstart](/forge/agents),
[function reference](/forge/functions), and [source-format map](/forge/design-systems).
They separate file intake from editing and rendering, and explain how to retain template,
revision, set and job identities between calls.
