Continuum connects to your social and ad accounts, builds a brand book from your website, and puts both to work. It analyzes what's performing, recommends the next move, generates on-brand creative, publishes it, and writes results back into the plan. Data in, better creative out.

You can use all of it from the AI assistant you already have open, or in the Continuum app.

## What Continuum is not

| You might expect | What it is instead |
| --- | --- |
| An AI ad generator | A loop. Creative is made from your performance data and brand book, and results flow back into the next plan. |
| An assistant that spends on its own | New campaigns are created paused, and budget moves wait for your approval. See [You stay in control](/mcp/control). |
| A brand compliance gate | The [brand readiness score](/brand/readiness) tells you how complete your brand book is. It never blocks anything. |
