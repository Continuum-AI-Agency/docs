Continuum uses a **connect once, assign to a brand** model. You connect a provider (like Meta) a single time at your account level, and Continuum discovers every asset you can access. You then assign the specific accounts — a Facebook Page, an Instagram profile, an ad account — to a given brand. Nothing syncs until you assign it, which keeps data from leaking between brands you manage.

## Supported channels

| Channel | What you get in Continuum |
| --- | --- |
| **Meta — Facebook** | Organic performance, plus your Meta ad accounts: campaign performance, creative, budgets, and reporting, with [Paid optimization](/performance/optimizer) and campaign management on the same connection. |
| **Meta — Instagram** | Organic performance, competitor discovery, and [publishing](/organic/publishing). |
| **LinkedIn** | Organic performance, and LinkedIn ads performance. Managing LinkedIn ads stays in LinkedIn for now. |
| **TikTok** | Organic performance. |
| **YouTube** | Organic channel performance. |

Instagram publishing is live. Publishing to Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, and YouTube is built, and your first post on each goes out supervised with the Continuum team. See [Publishing](/organic/publishing).

## Connecting a channel

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Integrations">
Go to **Settings → Integrations** and choose the provider you want to connect, such as Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok, or YouTube.
</Step>

<Step title="Authorize">
Complete the provider's OAuth flow in the popup. Continuum discovers every account and asset you can access — you don't authorize each one separately.
</Step>

<Step title="Assign accounts to a brand">
Pick the specific accounts that belong to this brand and assign them. Assigning starts background data collection for that account.
</Step>
</Steps>

## How Meta assets are organized

Meta nests deeply, so the asset picker mirrors its hierarchy: **Meta Business → ad accounts → sub-assets** (Pages and Instagram profiles). Selecting an ad account can surface the Instagram profiles tied to that business unit, so related assets stay together.

Assets that are discovered but not yet usable — for example, missing a permission — are flagged so you know what to fix before assigning them.

## Keeping connections healthy

Continuum tracks each connection's last sync and health. If a connection breaks — a password change or an expired token — the account is marked stale and shows a **Re-sync** action to restore data collection.

<Note>These are your ad and social platforms. AI assistants you've connected, such as Claude or ChatGPT, are listed under **Settings → Connections**. See [Connections and activity](/settings/connections).</Note>

## Try it

Ask Claude or ChatGPT:

- "Which platforms are connected to our brand?"
- "Connect our Instagram account."
- "Is our Meta connection healthy?"

When you ask to connect, you get a link to approve the connection. It works for 10 minutes.
