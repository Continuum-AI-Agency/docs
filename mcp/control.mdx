## What waits for your yes

Continuum shows you a preview of what will happen and waits for your yes before it:

- **Publishes** a post.
- **Creates, changes, or pauses** a campaign, ad set, ad, budget, or audience in your Meta account. Paid optimization never pauses an ad unless you approve that specific pause.
- **Swaps the creative** in a live ad. The swap can be undone.
- **Produces final versions** in Forge.
- **Deletes** something. The one exception is deleting a Forge render set, which has no preview step; your assistant asks you first.
- **Switches on** a campaign, ad set, or ad. New ones are always created paused. Switching one on needs an Admin or Owner to approve that exact change, in the Continuum app or from their own assistant with `mcp_approval_grant`. A large budget increase needs the same approval. If you're an Admin or Owner, your own yes is enough.

## What happens right away

| Change | Why it doesn't wait |
| --- | --- |
| Moving a post, rewriting a caption, marking work as needing changes, adding a comment | It stays inside your plan. Nothing is published or spent, but a scheduled post you move goes out at its new time, so check the date. |
| Generating on the Canvas, running a pipeline, editing or exporting video | You asked for the creative. Generation uses your plan's budget, and bigger jobs have [hourly limits](/mcp/limits) to protect it. |
| Approving or rejecting a Paid optimization recommendation | Your approval is the yes. |

<Tip>Say **"Show me first"** to preview any change before it's made.</Tip>

## What stays in the Continuum app

| Decision | Where it happens |
| --- | --- |
| Autopilot (automatic budget changes) | Only you can turn it on, from the Paid optimization dashboard. |
| Editing or deleting comments, collections, and share links | In the Continuum app. |

## Your access, and no more

The assistant can reach only the brands you belong to, and do only what your [role](/settings/roles) allows. It starts on the brand you last chose in your assistant, or otherwise your active brand in the app, and you can switch to any other brand you belong to by asking. Viewers can read results, plans, and the Library. Operators can also ask Jaina and the Organic agent, and create, change, and run content, creative, and campaigns. Admins and Owners can also manage people and settings, approve switching on ads, and produce final Forge versions.

## See everything it did

In Continuum, **Settings → Activity** lists every action you've taken through a connected assistant: the tool, whether it worked, and when. Each person sees their own activity. Continuum logs a fingerprint of what was asked, never the request itself. If something looks wrong, revoke the connection under **Settings → Connections** and contact [support@trycontinuum.ai](mailto:support@trycontinuum.ai).

## Disconnect at any time

In Continuum, **Settings → Connections** lists every assistant you've connected. Revoke any you no longer use.

## What your assistant sees

Claude or ChatGPT sees what you ask and what Continuum sends back to answer it, such as campaign figures, draft captions, and links to images. It never sees your Continuum password. That conversation follows your assistant's own privacy settings. Continuum's handling of your data is covered in our [privacy policy](https://app.trycontinuum.ai/privacy) and on the [Security](/security) page.

## Try it

- "Raise the daily budget on our retargeting ads to $150. Show me the change first."
- "Move Thursday's posts to Friday. Show me first."
- "Show me the paid changes waiting for my approval." (Admins and Owners)
