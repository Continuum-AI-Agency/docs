What to expect in each area today.

| Area | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| **Publishing** | Live on Instagram. For Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, and YouTube, contact support before your first post on each, and the Continuum team publishes it with you. TikTok posts publish as private until TikTok's app audit completes. See [Publishing](/organic/publishing). |
| **Ads** | Meta ads can be managed from your assistant. New campaigns, ad sets, and ads start paused. Switching one on, or a large budget increase, needs an Admin or Owner to approve that exact change, in the app or from their own assistant. |
| **Paid optimization** | Recommends budget moves for you to approve, and applies them when you do. It never pauses an ad unless you approve that specific pause. Your assistant can't switch a portfolio to Autopilot, which makes budget moves automatically; turn it on from your dashboard. |
| **Canvas** | Generate images and videos from your assistant. Up to 10 runs an hour for each brand. If the canvas is open in Continuum, you can watch the run happen. |
| **Pipelines** | Up to 10 versions per request. |
| **Live ads** | Swapping the creative in a live ad shows a preview first and can be undone. |
| **Video Studio** | Every edit is saved as a new version you can undo. Finished videos are saved to your Library. |
| **Forge** | You see a preview of every version before anything is made, and nothing is made until you confirm. Final versions need an Admin or Owner, even if you're an Operator. |
| **Library** | Find, review, comment on, and approve work from your assistant. Editing or deleting comments, collections, and share links are done in the Continuum app. |
| **Your role** | Set for each brand in Continuum. Viewers can read results, plans, and the Library. Operators can also ask Jaina and the Organic agent, and create, change, and run content, creative, and campaigns. Admins and Owners can also manage people and settings, approve switching on ads, and produce final Forge versions. See [Roles & permissions](/settings/roles). |

## Timing

Answers and reports usually take a minute or two. Images and video can take several minutes. Ask "Is it done yet?" to check, or come back to the same chat later.

## Limits

Hourly limits protect your budget. Most are set for each brand: besides Canvas runs, they cover publishing, approvals, ad changes, and trend discovery. Connection links are limited per person. If you hit one, Continuum says how long to wait. Ask for a smaller batch — three versions instead of ten — or try again later. The [server reference](/mcp/reference#limits) lists every limit.

## Try it

- "Run the product-reveal pipeline once for each of these ten headlines." One call queues up to ten versions.
- "Preview the budget change on our prospecting ad set, and wait for my yes before you apply it."
- "Which connections does this brand have, and which ones have lapsed?"
