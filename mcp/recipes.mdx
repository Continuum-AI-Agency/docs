Short sequences that show how everything connects. Use them as they are or adapt them. Send each line as its own message and read the answer before the next one.

## Monday morning review

<Steps>
<Step title="Compare the weeks">"Give me last week's social and ad results, compared with the week before."</Step>
<Step title="Find the why">"What were the best and worst performers, and why?"</Step>
<Step title="Pick one move">"What's the one change we should make this week?"</Step>
</Steps>

## Plan a launch week

<Steps>
<Step title="Load the context">"Read our brand book and the brief for the autumn launch."</Step>
<Step title="Find what works">"Which opening lines and formats have worked best for us?"</Step>
<Step title="Plan">"Plan launch week on Instagram using those."</Step>
<Step title="Make the creative">"Make the images for each post and show me the storyboards."</Step>
<Step title="Schedule">"Schedule the ones I approved."</Step>
</Steps>

## Refresh tired ads

<Steps>
<Step title="Find fatigue">"Which of our Meta ads are losing attention fastest?"</Step>
<Step title="Look outward">"What's working for competitors in the same space?"</Step>
<Step title="Make new versions">"Make three new versions of our weakest ad, using our best opening lines."</Step>
<Step title="Swap one in">"Swap the strongest new version into that ad. Show me first."</Step>
</Steps>

## Turn footage into social clips

<Steps>
<Step title="Cut">"Turn my latest video into three 15-second clips: a hook, a testimonial, and a demo."</Step>
<Step title="Finish">"Add on-brand captions and music to each."</Step>
<Step title="Plan">"Save them to the Library and add them to next week's posts."</Step>
</Steps>
