You don't need to know tool names to use Continuum. Your assistant picks them for you. This page is for admins reviewing what a connector can do and for anyone building with an agent.

The server registers **72 tools**. Your assistant's tool list (`tools/list`) shows up to **52** of them: the ones your [role](/settings/roles) and connected platforms allow. The other **20** are writes and helpers reached through the listed tools; they stay callable by name. `help_describe` with no arguments returns all 72.

<Info>Tools marked **Changes things** can modify your brand. The ones that publish, change your ads, produce final Forge versions, or delete something show a preview and wait for your yes. See [You stay in control](/mcp/control).</Info>

## For agents

- **Index:** [`/mcp/tools.json`](/mcp/tools.json) lists every registered tool with its role, connection, confirmation, completion, retry behavior, actions with required and optional inputs, an example call, and its errors. Generated from the server's own tool registry, as released on 2026-11-05.
- **Live schema:** call `help_describe {"tool": "<name>", "action": "<action>"}` before the first call to a tool. If it disagrees with these pages, follow the server.
- **Forge:** the [operation index](/forge/agent-manifest.json) and [function reference](/forge/functions) carry per-action contracts for `forge_query` and `forge_manage`.
- **How to behave:** read the [agent brief](/mcp/agent-brief) and the [server reference](/mcp/reference) for sign-in, brand scope, confirmations, statuses, and limits.
