**What the server tells your assistant:**

> What are the brand's tracked competitors doing? A digest over their PAID ads and ORGANIC posts in a window (default the last 90 days): one row per competitor × tag dimension (angle, hook, cta, format) × value, with paid \{ads, active, medianDaysRunning\} and organic \{posts, outlierShare = share of scored posts that earned ≥2× the account's median engagement\}, plus exemplarSnapshotIds (open with intel_competitor_ads snapshotId) and exemplarPostIds (the postId intel_competitor_organic returns). Rows with many long-running active ads are what a competitor keeps paying for; a high outlierShare is what lands organically. Call this FIRST for 'what angles/hooks/offers are competitors using', then open exemplars with the list tools. Read coverage before concluding: many untagged items mean the tagger has not reached them yet, not that competitors are quiet. trackedCompetitorCount 0 means nothing is tracked (intel_competitor_ads returns onboarding recommendations). Deterministic over stored tags — no model, no live Meta fetch. Narrow with competitorRef, dimensions, source (paid\|organic). Refresh or track new competitors with intel_competitor_scan. stillProcessing &gt; 0 means a scan is still analyzing that many ads: the result is partial, so present it as such and offer to check again shortly. Dimensions (one tag taxonomy shared by paid ads and organic posts): angle = the strategic selling angle the creative leads with; hook = how it opens; cta = what it asks the viewer to do (none = asks nothing); format = what kind of creative it is.
