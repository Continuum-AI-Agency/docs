sidebarTitle: "Connector issues"

---

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="I added the connector but can't see Continuum in my chat">
Check the Continuum connector is switched on for this chat, or start a new chat. On a Claude Team or Enterprise plan, your organization owner may need to add the connector first.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="It can't find my brand">
Ask "Which brands can I access?" If yours isn't listed, ask an Admin or Owner on that brand to invite you in Continuum.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I'm not sure which accounts are connected">
Ask "Which platforms are connected to our brand?" You can connect anything missing from the same chat.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="It asks me to pick a brand or connect an account">
That's normal. Answer the question, or open the link it gives you. Links work for 10 minutes.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="An account won't connect, or a post can't be published">
First ask "Is our Instagram connection healthy?" (or whichever platform). If the connection has lapsed, ask Continuum to reconnect it. If that platform doesn't support publishing from your assistant yet, Continuum says so. See [Good to know](/mcp/limits).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="It says my brand isn't ready">
Finish [setting up your brand](/brand/setup) in the Continuum app so your brand book can be built, then try again. Until then, you can still connect your accounts and look at your performance.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I'm not sure what the assistant just did">
Open **Settings → Activity** in Continuum. It lists every action you've taken through your assistant, and when. If something looks wrong, revoke the connection under **Settings → Connections** and contact [support@trycontinuum.ai](mailto:support@trycontinuum.ai).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="It says I don't have permission">
Your role doesn't allow that action. Ask an Admin or Owner on your brand. See [Roles & permissions](/settings/roles).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="It says my session has expired">
Remove the Continuum connector in your assistant's settings and add it again. You'll be asked to sign in to Continuum once more.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="ChatGPT won't finish signing in">
Check you used the ChatGPT address: `https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp/chatgpt`.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="It says I've hit a limit">
Bigger jobs, like generating creative, have hourly limits to protect your budget. Ask for a smaller batch, such as three versions instead of ten, or try again in about an hour. The message says how long to wait.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="It's taking a while">
Answers and reports usually take a minute or two. Images and video can take several minutes. Ask "Is it done yet?" to check, or come back to the same chat later.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still stuck?

Email [support@trycontinuum.ai](mailto:support@trycontinuum.ai) with the assistant you're using and what you asked.
