Manage your Meta ads from the conversation. Review campaigns, adjust budgets, pause or update campaigns, ad sets, and ads, build audiences (including lookalikes), and set up new campaigns. New campaigns are always created **paused**, so nothing spends until you switch it on in Continuum.

[Paid optimization](/performance/optimizer) spots where your budget would work harder and recommends the move, with the reasoning. You approve or reject each one.

## Prompts to try

- "Show me this week's budget recommendations and explain each one."
- "Approve the recommendations for our prospecting campaigns, and reject the rest."
- "Raise the daily budget on our retargeting ads to $150. Show me the change first."
- "Pause the ad sets with the highest cost per purchase. Show me first."
- "Build a new audience of people similar to our past purchasers in the US."
- "Set up a paused campaign to drive traffic to our spring sale."

## Good to know

- Creating or changing campaigns, ad sets, ads, budgets, and audiences shows a preview and waits for your yes.
- Approving a Paid optimization recommendation applies it right away. Your approval is the yes.
- You switch campaigns on in the Continuum app, not from your assistant.
- Paid optimization recommends budget moves for you to approve. It never pauses an ad. Autopilot, which makes budget moves automatically, can only be turned on from your dashboard.
