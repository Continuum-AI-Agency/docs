Keep every piece of writing on-brand. Ask about your [brand book](/brand/overview), guidelines, and audience, check copy before it goes out, and add new material for Continuum to learn from.

## Prompts to try

- "What does our brand book say about how we talk on LinkedIn?"
- "Check this caption against our brand voice: 'Big news!!! Our new blend is HERE and it's insane.'"
- "Who is our core customer, in two sentences?"
- "Save this product FAQ so you can use it in future posts."

<Tip>Start a session with "Read our brand book." Everything the assistant writes after that is grounded in your voice.</Tip>

## Good to know

- If your brand isn't ready yet, finish [setting up your brand](/brand/setup) in the Continuum app so your brand book can be built. Until then, you can still connect accounts and look at your performance.
- Material you save is added to your brand's [knowledge base](/settings/knowledge), where it also feeds the agents in the app.
