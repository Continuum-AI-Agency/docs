Turn one idea into on-brand images and videos, then find, review, and approve them in one place.

- **Find anything** in your [Library](/canvas/library) by describing it, and see where each creative has run and which version performs best.
- **Build new creative on the [Canvas](/canvas/overview)** while you watch, then add it to a scheduled post or swap it into a live ad.
- **Make several versions at once with [Pipelines](/canvas/pipelines)**, so you can test different ideas side by side.
- **Keep your product or model consistent** across everything you make with [Elements](/canvas/elements).
- **Review work together**: leave feedback on a specific spot in an image or moment in a video, and mark work as approved.

## Prompts to try

- "Find our approved product shots on a white background."
- "Which version of this ad creative is winning, and where is it running?"
- "Turn this product photo into three vertical lifestyle images for Reels."
- "Run our customer-testimonial pipeline with these three angles: taste, price, convenience."
- "Put the finished image on next Tuesday's post."
- "Mark this image as needs changes, and leave a note about the logo placement."

## Good to know

- Generation starts right away, without a preview. The Canvas allows up to 10 runs an hour for each brand, and a pipeline makes up to 10 versions per request. If you hit a limit, ask for a smaller batch or try again later.
- If the canvas is open in Continuum, you can watch the assistant build and run it.
- Swapping the creative in a live ad shows a preview first and can be undone.
- You can find, review, comment on, and approve work from your assistant. Editing or deleting comments, collections, and share links are done in the Continuum app.
