Base your next post or ad on evidence. Continuum studies your best posts and ads to find the openings, angles, and formats that work for you. It also tracks trends in your category and what your competitors are running.

## Prompts to try

- "Which opening lines work best for us, and what's the proof?"
- "Score this opening line before I post it: 'Stop scrolling if you hate Mondays.'"
- "What's trending in our category this week, and what could we post about it?"
- "Which competitor ads have been running the longest? What does that tell us?"
- "Where are competitors winning that we aren't even showing up?"
