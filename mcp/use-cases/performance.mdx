Know what's working, what isn't, and why, across your social channels and your ads. For deeper questions, ask [Jaina](/performance/jaina) about ads or the [Organic agent](/organic/agent) about social.

## What you can read

| Area | Platforms |
| --- | --- |
| **Organic social** | Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn |
| **Ad accounts** | Meta and LinkedIn |
| **Website** | Google Analytics (GA4) |

Data appears for each account connected and assigned to your brand. Ask "Which platforms are connected to our brand?" to check, and connect anything missing from the same chat.

## Prompts to try

- "What were our three best Instagram posts last month, and why did they work?"
- "Ask Jaina why our cost per purchase jumped last week."
- "Which Meta campaigns are spending slower than planned this month?"
- "Break down the last two weeks of Meta spend by age and gender. Where are we wasting money?"
- "Which of our video ads hold attention past the first three seconds, and which lose people?"
- "Give me a one-page weekly review of social and ads, with the single most important action."

<Tip>Answers and reports usually take a minute or two. Ask "Is it done yet?" to check, or come back to the same chat later.</Tip>
