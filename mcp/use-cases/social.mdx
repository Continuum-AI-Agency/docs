Plan a week of posts in one conversation, then review each one before it goes out. Write the posts, make the images and videos, and publish. Your [Calendar](/organic/calendar) in Continuum updates as you go.

## Where you can publish

| Platform | From your assistant |
| --- | --- |
| **Instagram** | Plan, track, and publish |
| **Facebook**, **LinkedIn** | Plan and track. Publishing is coming next. |
| **Other platforms** | Plan and track |

## Prompts to try

- "Plan next week's Instagram content around our autumn launch."
- "What's ready to post right now?"
- "Move all of Thursday's posts to Friday."
- "Rewrite this caption in Mexican Spanish and make it more playful."
- "Make the images for Tuesday's post. Show me the storyboard first."
- "Show me Tuesday's approved carousel as it will post, then publish it."

## Good to know

- Publishing always shows you the post as it will go out and waits for your yes.
- Moving a post or rewriting a caption happens right away. A scheduled post you move goes out at its new time, so check the date.
- Your Instagram needs to be a Business or Creator account to connect.
