A **template** is a design your team has already set up, such as a promo video or a story card. **[Forge](/forge/overview)** fills it with your messages, products, and offers to make many on-brand versions in one go, from a brief or your documents. You preview every version before anything is made.

## Prompts to try

- "Which templates do we have?"
- "Write six versions of the promo template from this brief."
- "Produce the whole set. Show me what will be made first."

## Good to know

- You see a preview of every version, and nothing is made until you confirm.
- Final versions, rather than draft previews, need an **Admin** or **Owner**, even if you're an **Operator**.
