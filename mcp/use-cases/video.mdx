Turn raw footage into first cuts for social from your conversation. Every change shows up live in [Video Studio](/canvas/video-studio), and you can undo any of them.

## Prompts to try

- "Cut the pauses from my latest video and add captions."
- "Turn this footage into three 30-second customer testimonial cuts."
- "Cut it to the beat of the music."
- "Add a headline at the start and a call to action at the end."
- "Reformat it for Reels and save it to the Library."

## Good to know

- Every edit is saved as a new version you can undo.
- Finished videos are saved to your [Library](/canvas/library).
