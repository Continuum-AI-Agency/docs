Organic analytics shows how your posted content performs across every connected platform, so you can see what's working and feed it back into planning.

## What you'll see

- **Per-platform performance** — organic results across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn, side by side.
- **Audience location** — where your audience is, shown on a map.
- **Content performance** — how individual posts are doing over time.

Data appears for each platform you've connected and assigned to this brand. See [Integrations](/integrations) to connect more.

## Closing the loop

Use what you learn here to steer the next round: hand the [Organic agent](/organic/agent) your best-performing themes, and build more drafts around them in the [calendar](/organic/calendar).
