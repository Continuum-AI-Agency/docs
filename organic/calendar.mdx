The content calendar — the **Planner** — is where organic content gets planned and scheduled. It starts from brand-relevant signals and turns them into **drafts** you refine, approve, and schedule across platforms.

## Choose a view

Switch between **Week**, **Month**, and **List** to plan at the cadence you want.

## Seed it with trends and signals

Open the **Trends & Signals** drawer and choose **Generate Trends** to pull what's relevant to your niche right now. Filter by:

- **Events** — seasonal and calendar moments worth posting around.
- **Questions** — what your audience is actually asking.
- **Rising momentum** — trends that are picking up.

Pick the ones worth acting on and turn them into drafts.

## Add posts

| Option | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **New post** | A single-image or video post. |
| **New carousel** | A multi-slide post. |
| **New reel** | A short vertical video. |
| **AI one-shot post** | Describe the post and the AI writes and makes it for the day and platform you picked. |

The [Organic agent](/organic/agent) can also fill the calendar with a plan in one go.

## Draft statuses

The main path a post takes:

<Flow
  label="A post's main path"
  steps={["Concept", "Generating", "Draft", "Scheduled", "Published"]}
/>

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Concept` | A planned idea. Nothing has been generated yet. |
| `Seeded` | A slot is created and waiting for generation. |
| `Queued` | Waiting for a generation slot. |
| `Generating` | Being generated right now. |
| `Draft` | Written, and not yet approved for posting. |
| `Scheduled` | Approved. It will post at the scheduled time. |
| `Published` | Live on the platform. |
| `Media failed` | The copy is saved, but the media didn't render. |
| `Failed` | Generation or publishing didn't complete. See [Publishing](/organic/publishing). |
| `Cancelled` | Stopped before it finished. |

## Approve and schedule

Edit each draft — caption, format, creative, and slot — then choose **Approve & schedule**. The draft moves to `Scheduled` and posts at its scheduled time.

<Tip>Made something on the canvas? A [Planner Draft node](/canvas/nodes/delivery) puts it straight onto a draft, and a canvas workspace opened from the Planner has **Apply Back to Planner**.</Tip>
