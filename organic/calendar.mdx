The content calendar — the Planner — is where organic content gets scheduled. It starts from brand-relevant insights and turns them into **drafts** you can generate, refine, and publish across platforms.

## Insights that seed content

The calendar surfaces three kinds of prompts, tuned to your brand's niche:

- **Trends** — what's rising, stable, or cooling in your space.
- **Key events** — seasonal and calendar moments worth posting around.
- **Audience questions** — what your audience is actually asking.

Select the ones worth acting on and turn them into drafts.

## Planning and scheduling

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick your views">
Switch between **week**, **month**, and **list** views to plan at the cadence you want.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose what to post about">
Select trends, events, or audience questions to build drafts from.
</Step>

<Step title="Generate drafts">
Create drafts for the platforms you're targeting. A ticker shows generation progress as drafts come in.
</Step>

<Step title="Refine and schedule">
Edit each draft, set its slot, and schedule it. Video made in [AI Studio](/studio/overview) can be published straight here with a Publish to Planner node.
</Step>
</Steps>
