Organic is your workspace for unpaid social. It turns brand insights into scheduled posts and tells you how they performed. Three areas work together:

  See how posted content performed.
</Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>In the sidebar these read **Organic Agent**, **Calendar**, and **Organic Analytics**. Inside the workspace the tabs read **Agent**, **Planner**, and **Metrics** — same surfaces.</Note>

## How it fits together

The **agent** proposes plans and concepts and drops them into the **calendar** as drafts. You schedule and publish from the calendar, and **analytics** shows the results — which feed your next round of planning. Creative made in [AI Studio](/studio/overview) can be published straight into the calendar with a Publish to Planner node.
