## How it fits together

<Steps>
<Step title="Find the angle">
Trends, key events, and audience questions in your niche seed ideas. The [Organic agent](/organic/agent) turns them into a plan.
</Step>
<Step title="Plan and draft">
Drafts land in the [calendar](/organic/calendar), where you edit captions, formats, and creative. Creative from the [AI Canvas](/canvas/overview) can go straight onto a draft with a [Planner Draft node](/canvas/nodes/delivery).
</Step>
<Step title="Approve and publish">
Approve and schedule a post, and Continuum [publishes](/organic/publishing) it.
</Step>
<Step title="Learn">
[Organic analytics](/organic/analytics) shows what worked, and the agent uses it when it plans the next round.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Try it

Ask Claude or ChatGPT:

- "Plan next week's Instagram content around our autumn launch."
- "What's ready to post right now?"
- "Ask the Organic agent which content themes we should lean into next month."

You can also rewrite captions, move posts, and publish to Instagram from your assistant. See [Plan and publish social content](/mcp/use-cases/social).
