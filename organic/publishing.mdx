Publishing runs from inside Continuum: you approve a post in the Planner and Continuum publishes it. It's approval-first by design — nothing goes out until a person approves it.

## Where you can publish

| Platform | Status |
| --- | --- |
| **Instagram** | Live. |
| **Facebook**, **LinkedIn**, **TikTok**, **YouTube** | Built, and going through each platform's own audit and usage approval. We'll put your first post on each one out with you, supervised. |
| **X** | Shown in the Planner as coming soon. |

<Note>Until TikTok's audit completes, posts to TikTok publish as private.</Note>

You can plan and track posts for every platform in the [calendar](/organic/calendar), whether or not it publishes from Continuum yet.

## Publish a post

<Steps>
<Step title="Finish the draft">
Edit the caption, format, creative, and slot in the [Planner](/organic/calendar).
</Step>
<Step title="Approve and schedule">
Choose **Approve & schedule**. The post's status changes to `Scheduled`.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the result">
When it goes out, the status changes to `Published`. If it didn't, it shows `Failed`.
</Step>
</Steps>

From the canvas, a [Post to Platform node](/canvas/nodes/delivery) can **Post now** or **Schedule for its planner time**.

## If a post fails

- Check the connection is healthy under **Settings → Integrations**. A lapsed connection shows a **Re-sync** action. See [Integrations](/integrations).
- `Media failed` means the copy is saved but the media didn't render. Regenerate or replace the media.

## From your AI assistant

Ask Claude or ChatGPT to "Show me Tuesday's approved carousel as it will post, then publish it." It always shows the post as it will go out and waits for your yes. Publishing from your assistant is available for Instagram today. See [Plan and publish social content](/mcp/use-cases/social).
