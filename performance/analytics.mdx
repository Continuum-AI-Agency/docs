Paid analytics is your dashboard for everything running. It brings your connected ad accounts into one workspace so you can see performance, pacing, and where to act.

<Note>The sidebar labels this **Paid Analytics**; inside Performance+ it's the **Dashboard** tab.</Note>

## What's on the dashboard

- **Campaign and ad-set workspace** — performance across the ad accounts assigned to this brand.
- **Budget pacing** — how spend is tracking against plan, so you catch over- and under-pacing early.
- **Pending and Activity** — actions waiting on you, and what's been done. Open **Full surface** for the full [approvals](/performance/approvals) view.
- **Insight panels** — account- and campaign-level breakdowns of what's driving results.

Data appears for each ad account you've connected and assigned. See [Integrations](/integrations) to add more.

## From insight to action

When you want to go deeper, ask [Jaina](/performance/jaina) about anything on the dashboard, or move to [Paid optimization](/performance/optimizer) to apply recommendations.
