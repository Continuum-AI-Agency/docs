---
title: "Approvals"
description: "Review and sign off on creatives and campaigns before they go live."
---

Approvals give paid work a review step. Before creatives or campaigns go live, they can route through an approval so the right people sign off first. Open them from the **Pending** widget on the [paid analytics](/performance/analytics) dashboard by selecting **Full surface**.

## How it works

- Work that needs review shows up in the approvals queue.
- Reviewers approve or send items back with feedback.
- Approved work is cleared to proceed.

This keeps a check between building a campaign and launching it, which matters when more than one person touches paid.

<Info>Approvals are part of [Performance+](/performance/overview). See [pricing](https://trycontinuum.ai/#pricing).</Info>
