Changes to your ad account wait for a person to approve that exact change. "The Optimizer proposes. Jaina explains. You approve." The one exception is [Autopilot](/performance/optimizer#modes), which you turn on for a portfolio and which applies budget moves inside caps you set. Here's what waits for a yes, and who can give it.

## What waits for a yes

| What | Where you approve | Who can approve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A [Paid optimization](/performance/optimizer) budget recommendation | **Paid Optimization → Actions**, or from your assistant | **Operator** or higher |
| A move [Jaina](/performance/jaina) proposes — a new audience, creative angles, a creative slate, a pipeline run, or an Optimizer approval | In the chat with Jaina, on that exact action | You, in the chat |
| Creating or changing a Meta campaign, ad set, ad, budget, or audience from your assistant | Your assistant shows a preview and waits for your yes | **Operator** or higher |
| Switching on a paused campaign, ad set, or ad, or a large budget increase | In the Continuum app, or from an Admin's or Owner's own assistant with `mcp_approval_grant` | **Admin** or **Owner** |

New campaigns, ad sets, and ads are always created **paused**. The Optimizer never pauses anything without a person approving that specific pause.

<Info>Approvals are part of [Performance+](/performance/overview). See [pricing](https://trycontinuum.ai/#pricing).</Info>

## Try it

In the app, go to **Paid Optimization → Actions**, open a recommendation, read its reasoning, and approve or reject it.

Or ask Claude or ChatGPT:

- "Show me this week's budget recommendations and explain each one."
- "Approve the recommendations for our prospecting campaigns, and reject the rest."
- "Show me the paid changes waiting for my approval." (Admins and Owners)
