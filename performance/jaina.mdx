Jaina is your paid-media analyst. Ask a question in plain language — "why did ROAS drop last week?" — and Jaina analyzes your connected accounts and returns a structured **checkpoint report** rather than a wall of text.

## What a report contains

- **KPI metrics** — the numbers that matter for your question, laid out as a grid.
- **Charts** — visual trends behind the numbers.
- **Strategic insights** — what the data means.
- **Prioritized recommendations** — concrete next steps, each tagged with **impact** and **effort** so you know what to do first.

## Where to use Jaina

- As the **Jaina** tab in [Performance+](/performance/overview), with a side canvas and full-screen mode for deeper sessions.
- From Claude or ChatGPT, through the connector. See [Understand your performance](/mcp/use-cases/performance).

## When Jaina proposes a move

Jaina can also propose the next move — a new audience, a set of creative angles, a creative slate, a pipeline run, or an Optimizer approval. Nothing touches your ad account or spends anything until you approve that exact action in the chat.

<Tip>Jaina reads the ad accounts assigned to your active brand. Connect and assign accounts in [Integrations](/integrations) first.</Tip>

## Try it

In the **Jaina** tab, ask the question directly: "Why did ROAS drop last week?"

From Claude or ChatGPT, ask:

- "Ask Jaina why our cost per purchase jumped last week."
- "Ask Jaina where to cut $2,000 from next week's Meta spend."
- "Ask Jaina for a weekly report on our Meta ads, with the top three actions."

Asking Jaina from your assistant needs the **Operator** role or higher.
