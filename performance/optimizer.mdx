Paid optimization — the Optimizer — watches ad-set performance daily and proposes budget moves with the reasoning attached. You approve them. It works across **portfolios** of ad sets rather than one campaign at a time.

<Note>The sidebar labels this **Paid Optimization**. Inside Performance+ it's the **Optimization** tab.</Note>

## How it's organized

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The state of optimization across your portfolios at a glance. |
| **Portfolios** | Your portfolios, their ad sets, and their pending recommendations. |
| **Actions** | Recommendations to review, approve, or reject. |
| **Activity** | A history of what's been actioned. |

## Modes

Each portfolio runs in one of three modes:

| Mode | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Observe** | The Optimizer watches and reports. |
| **Recommend** | It proposes budget moves for you to approve or reject. |
| **Autopilot** | It applies moves automatically, inside caps you set. |

Autopilot is opt-in for each portfolio. You can arm it only once both guardrail caps are set — a maximum daily spend change and a maximum change per cycle — and anything it creates starts paused. It never pauses anything without you approving that specific pause.

<Tip>Start in **Recommend** and run the first few cycles yourself. Turn on Autopilot only from the dashboard; it can't be turned on from your AI assistant.</Tip>

## Working with recommendations

Review each recommendation and its reasoning under **Actions**, then approve or reject it. Everything that's been done appears under **Activity**. If a portfolio isn't set up or an account is offline, the Optimizer tells you what to connect first.
