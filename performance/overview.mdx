## The three tabs

In the sidebar, Performance+ shows **Jaina**, **Paid Analytics**, and **Paid Optimization**. Inside the workspace they're the **Jaina**, **Dashboard**, and **Optimization** tabs.

## What's supported

| Platform | In Performance+ |
| --- | --- |
| **Meta** | Performance, creative, budgets, and reporting, with optimization and campaign management on the same connection. |
| **LinkedIn** | Paid performance is visible. Managing LinkedIn ads stays in LinkedIn for now. |

New campaigns, ad sets, and ads Continuum creates are always **paused** until an Admin or Owner approves switching them on.

## Try it

Everything here is also reachable from Claude or ChatGPT. Ask:

- "Ask Jaina why our cost per purchase jumped last week."
- "Show me this week's budget recommendations and explain each one."
- "Set up a paused campaign to drive traffic to our spring sale."
