Continuum's analytics engine gives you a unified view of creative and campaign performance across every channel.

## Dashboard overview

The analytics dashboard shows:

- **Campaign performance** — impressions, clicks, conversions, and spend across all active campaigns.
- **Creative leaderboard** — your top-performing assets ranked by engagement, CTR, or ROAS.
- **Channel breakdown** — side-by-side comparison of performance by platform.
- **Trend analysis** — performance over time so you can spot patterns and seasonal effects.

## Key metrics

| Metric | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **ROAS** | Return on ad spend — revenue generated per dollar spent. |
| **CTR** | Click-through rate — percentage of impressions that result in a click. |
| **Conversion rate** | Percentage of clicks that result in a desired action. |
| **Engagement rate** | Likes, comments, shares, and saves relative to reach. |
| **Brand score** | Average brand compliance score across all published creatives. |
| **Production velocity** | Number of creatives produced per time period. |

## Creative insights

Continuum goes beyond standard metrics by analyzing **what makes your content work**:

- **Element analysis** — which visual elements (colors, layouts, CTAs) correlate with higher performance.
- **Copy analysis** — which messaging themes and tones drive the most engagement.
- **Format performance** — how different content formats (static, video, carousel) compare.

<Tip>Use creative insights to inform your template library — double down on the formats and styles that your audience responds to.</Tip>

## Automated reports

Set up scheduled reports delivered to your inbox or shared with stakeholders:

1. Go to **Analytics → Reports**.
2. Select the metrics and campaigns to include.
3. Choose a cadence — daily, weekly, or monthly.
4. Add recipients.

<Info>Reports are exported as PDF or CSV. You can also connect Continuum to your BI tools via webhook.</Info>
