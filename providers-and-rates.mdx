Image, video, voiceover, and music generation is paid for with the brand's **Canvas credits**. The rates are the same wherever you generate: the AI Canvas, Pipelines, Video Studio, your assistant, Organic+ drafts, and Jaina's ad creatives.

## How credits are counted

- **Images** are charged per image.
- **Video** is charged per second of footage.
- **Voiceovers** are charged by the length of the speech, and **music** per track.
- A generation that fails or is refused costs nothing.
- Each run's total is rounded up to a whole credit. A run of several images can cost a credit less than the table adds up to.
- Each generation spends rollover credits first, then this month's included credits, then purchased packs.

## Video models

| Model | Made by | Credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Omni 1.1 Flash** (default) | Google | 11.5 per second, rounded up per clip |

An 8-second clip costs 92 credits, and a 10-second clip costs 115. You pay for the seconds the model actually renders. **Omni 1.1 Flash (Edit)** and **Extend Video** run on Omni 1.1 Flash at the same rate.

<Info>Google retired Veo 3.1. If you pick **Veo 3.1**, **Veo 3.1 Fast**, or **Veo 3.1 Lite**, the clip is made with Omni 1.1 Flash and costs the same as Omni.</Info>

**Returning soon:** Kling Omni (Kuaishou), Pixverse V6 (PixVerse), and Seedance 2.0 (ByteDance).

## Voiceovers and music

| Model | Made by | Used for | Credits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS** | Google | Voiceovers | About 1 per 19 seconds of speech, rounded up per voiceover |
| **Lyria 3.5** | Google | Music beds and songs | 10 per track |

A 30-second voiceover costs 2 credits, and a 60-second one costs 4. Voiceovers and music made for your videos in Video Studio, Organic+ reels, and Jaina's ad creatives use these rates. When an edit needs a track with a steady beat to cut to, more than one track can be made, and each one is charged.

## What doesn't use credits

- Forge renders.
- Conversations with the Organic agent and Jaina.
- Jaina's reports and recommendations.

<Tip>**Run Flow** skips nodes that haven't changed since their last run, so you aren't charged for them again. **Rerun all** regenerates every node.</Tip>

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Images per Image Generation run | Up to 4 |
| Canvas runs from your assistant | 10 an hour per brand |
| Generation nodes in one assistant run | Up to 6 |
| Pipeline versions per request | Up to 10 |

The [server reference](/mcp/reference#limits) lists every limit that applies when you work from your assistant. If a brand runs out of credits, see [Billing](/settings/billing).

Rates can change when a provider changes its pricing. This page shows the current rates. For plans and credit packs, see [pricing](https://trycontinuum.ai/#pricing).
