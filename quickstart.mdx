Get up and running with Continuum in three steps.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create your account">

Head to [app.trycontinuum.ai](https://app.trycontinuum.ai/signup) and sign up with your work email. You'll be prompted to name your workspace and invite your team.

<Tip>You can invite teammates at any time from **Settings → Team**.</Tip>

</Step>

<Step title="Set up your brand">

Upload your brand assets and configure your guardrails so every creative stays on-brand from day one.

1. Go to **Brand → Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Upload your logo, select brand colors, and define your tone of voice.
3. Add any compliance rules or restricted terms.

Your brand profile powers Continuum's AI — it uses these guidelines every time it generates or reviews content.

<Info>Need help configuring guardrails? See [Brand guardrails](/platform/brand-guardrails).</Info>

</Step>

<Step title="Launch your first campaign">

Create a campaign to start producing and scheduling content.

1. Click **+ New Campaign** from the dashboard.
2. Choose your channels (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, etc.).
3. Select a template or start from scratch in the Canvas Studio.
4. Set your schedule and let Continuum handle the rest.

<Tip>Continuum's AI will suggest optimal posting times based on your audience data.</Tip>

</Step>
</Steps>
