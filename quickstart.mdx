title: "Quickstart"
description: "Set up your first brand from your website and launch into Continuum in a few minutes."
---

Continuum builds your brand from your website. You give it a URL, it scans your site to extract your brand identity, and you review and launch. The whole flow takes a few minutes.

<Steps>
<Step title="Enter your website">

Head to [app.trycontinuum.ai](https://app.trycontinuum.ai) and start onboarding. Enter your website URL and select **Analyze my brand**.

Continuum scans your site and starts an AI analysis in the background — extracting your name, logo, colors, typography, voice, and audience while you continue.

</Step>

<Step title="Add brand documents (optional)">

Upload any brand assets you already have — style guides, decks, or past campaigns. This sharpens the analysis, but it's optional: skip it and Continuum infers everything from your website.

</Step>

<Step title="Connect your channels">

Tag your ad and social accounts to this brand. Connect a provider once, then assign the specific accounts that belong to this brand.

- **Meta** — Facebook, Instagram, and Threads
- **Google** — Google Ads, Google Analytics (GA4), and YouTube
- **TikTok**

Connecting Instagram unlocks competitor inspiration later. You can add more channels — including LinkedIn — anytime from **Settings → Integrations**. See [Integrations](/integrations) for the full list.

</Step>

<Step title="Invite your team (optional)">

Invite teammates and set each one's role — **Admin**, **Operator**, or **Viewer**. You can also do this later from Settings.

<Tip>Not sure which role to give? See [Roles & permissions](/settings/roles).</Tip>

</Step>

<Step title="Review your Brand DNA and launch">

Continuum shows the brand profile it extracted — identity (logo, colors, typography), business overview, voice and tone, target audience, and a **brand readiness score**. Edit anything that's off, then launch.

The readiness score tells you how complete your brand profile is. It's informational — it never blocks you. You can deepen your [brand book](/brand/overview) over time.

<Info>After you launch, Continuum can show competitor inspiration and generate your first on-brand creatives, then drop you on your Home dashboard.</Info>

</Step>
</Steps>
