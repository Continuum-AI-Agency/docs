The fastest way to use Continuum is from the AI assistant you already have open. Connect it once, then ask in your own words.

<Info>New to Continuum? [Set up your brand](/brand/setup) in the app first. It builds your brand book from your website in a few minutes, and everything your assistant writes draws on it.</Info>

## Pick your path

**You use Claude.** Go to **Settings → Connectors**, choose **Add custom connector**, and paste `https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp`. Click **Connect**, sign in to Continuum, and click **Allow access**.

**You use ChatGPT.** Switch on **Developer mode** under **Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings**, choose **Create**, and paste `https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp/chatgpt`. Sign in to Continuum and click **Allow access**.

**You use another assistant.** Add `https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp` as a custom connector and sign in.

Full steps for each assistant are in [Connect your assistant](/mcp/connect).

## Your first five minutes

Start with these, in order. Each one builds on the last.

<Steps>
<Step title="Check where you are">
> "Which Continuum brand am I working on, and what's connected?"

Continuum starts on the brand you last chose in your assistant, or otherwise your active brand in the app. To change it, say "Switch to Acme Coffee."
</Step>
<Step title="Connect your channels">
> "Connect our Instagram account."

You get a link to approve the connection. Your Instagram needs to be a Business or Creator account; if it's a personal account, switch it in the Instagram app's settings. Do the same for your Meta ads and any other platform you use.
</Step>
<Step title="Ground everything in your brand">
> "Read our brand book and tell me in three lines how we sound."

From here on, everything the assistant writes is grounded in your voice.
</Step>
<Step title="See how you're doing">
> "How did we do last week on Instagram and on our Meta ads?"
</Step>
<Step title="Decide what's next">
> "Based on that, what's the one thing we should do this week?"
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Not sure what to ask? Try "What can you help me with in the Calendar?" It's a good way to explore any part of Continuum.</Tip>

## Or hand it one prompt

Paste this into a new chat once the connector is on. Your assistant runs the five steps above and stops to ask you before connecting anything.

```text Paste into Claude or ChatGPT wrap
Use the Continuum connector. Tell me which brand I'm on and which platforms are
connected. If Instagram or Meta ads aren't connected, give me the links to connect
them and wait. Then read our brand book and summarize how we sound in three lines,
show me last week's results on Instagram and Meta ads, and finish with the one thing
we should do this week.
```
