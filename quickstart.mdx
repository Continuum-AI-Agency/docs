## Before you start

| You need | Why |
| --- | --- |
| A Continuum login with access to a brand | Your assistant always works on one brand. If a teammate invited you, accept the email invite. If you're new, see **No brand yet?** below. |
| An assistant that allows custom connectors | Claude (web, desktop, or mobile), ChatGPT with **Developer mode**, or any MCP client. On a Claude Team or Enterprise plan, your organization owner may need to add the connector first. |
| The right role for what you'll do | **Viewer** can read results: analytics, the brand book, the calendar, and the Library. Asking Jaina or the Organic agent, drafting, publishing, generating, or changing ads needs **Operator** or higher. See [Roles](/settings/roles). |
| The right plan for what you'll use | Connecting an assistant needs no plan of its own. Organic+ and Performance+ each need their plan on the brand, set under **Settings → Billing** (see [pricing](https://trycontinuum.ai/#pricing)), and Creative+ generation draws on the brand's Canvas credits. In the app, a product line that isn't on the brand's plan shows as locked. |

### No brand yet?

<Steps>
<Step title="Start onboarding">
Go to [app.trycontinuum.ai](https://app.trycontinuum.ai), enter your website URL, and select **Analyze my brand**. Continuum builds your brand book from the site in a few minutes.
</Step>
<Step title="Review and launch">
Check what it found (name, logo, colors, typography, voice, and audience), then launch. You can connect channels and invite your team now or later. The full flow is in [Set up your brand](/brand/setup).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Server addresses

| Assistant | Address |
| --- | --- |
| Claude, and every other assistant that supports custom connectors | `https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp` |
| ChatGPT | `https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp/chatgpt` |

<Warning>ChatGPT needs its own address. If ChatGPT won't finish signing in, check that you used the `/mcp/chatgpt` address.</Warning>

## Connect your assistant

### Claude

<Steps>
<Step title="Add the connector">
Go to **Settings → Connectors**, choose **Add custom connector**, name it `Continuum`, and paste `https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp`.
</Step>
<Step title="Sign in">
Click **Connect**, sign in to Continuum, pick the brand to connect, and click **Allow access**.
</Step>
</Steps>

The connector also works in the Claude mobile app. On a Claude Team or Enterprise plan, your organization owner may need to add it before you can use it. If you don't see **Add custom connector**, your Claude plan or organization doesn't allow custom connectors.

### ChatGPT

<Steps>
<Step title="Turn on developer mode">
Go to **Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings** and switch on **Developer mode**. You only do this once. If you don't see **Developer mode**, your ChatGPT plan or workspace admin doesn't allow custom connectors.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the connector">
Choose **Create**, name it `Continuum`, and paste `https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp/chatgpt`. ChatGPT needs this address, not the plain `/mcp` one.
</Step>
<Step title="Sign in">
Sign in to Continuum, pick the brand to connect, and click **Allow access**.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Claude Code

<Visibility for="humans">
```bash
claude mcp add --transport http continuum https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp
```
</Visibility>

<Visibility for="agents">
Run `claude mcp add --transport http continuum https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp`.
</Visibility>

Then run `/mcp` inside Claude Code, choose `continuum`, and sign in to Continuum in the browser window it opens.

### Cursor

Add Continuum to `~/.cursor/mcp.json` to use it in every project, or to `.cursor/mcp.json` for one project:

<Visibility for="humans">
```json ~/.cursor/mcp.json
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "continuum": {
      "url": "https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp"
    }
  }
}
```
</Visibility>

<Visibility for="agents">
In `~/.cursor/mcp.json`: `{"mcpServers":{"continuum":{"url":"https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp"}}}`
</Visibility>

Open **Cursor Settings → MCP**, find `continuum`, and sign in to Continuum when Cursor asks.

### VS Code

Run **MCP: Add Server** from the Command Palette and choose **HTTP**, or add Continuum to `.vscode/mcp.json`:

<Visibility for="humans">
```json .vscode/mcp.json
{
  "servers": {
    "continuum": {
      "type": "http",
      "url": "https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp"
    }
  }
}
```
</Visibility>

<Visibility for="agents">
In `.vscode/mcp.json`: `{"servers":{"continuum":{"type":"http","url":"https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp"}}}`
</Visibility>

The first time Copilot uses it, VS Code opens a browser window to sign in to Continuum.

<Visibility for="agents">
Run `codex mcp add continuum --url https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp`, then `codex mcp login continuum`.

### Any other assistant

Any assistant that supports custom (remote) MCP connectors can use `https://api.trycontinuum.ai/mcp`. Add it as a remote server, then sign in to Continuum in the browser window it opens.

## Check that it worked

Start a new chat and ask:

> "Which Continuum brand am I working on, and what's connected?"

You should get the brand's name and a list of its connected platforms. If your assistant says it has no Continuum tools, or sign-in keeps failing, see [Assistant troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#in-your-ai-assistant).

## What you're allowing

When you click **Allow access**, your assistant works in Continuum as you: only on your brands, only within your [role](/settings/roles), and with a preview before anything goes live. [You stay in control](/mcp/control) has the details, and [Connections and activity](/settings/connections) shows how to disconnect later.

## Your first five minutes

Start with these, in order. Each one builds on the last.

<Steps>
<Step title="Check where you are">
> "Which Continuum brand am I working on, and what's connected?"

You get the brand's name and what's connected. Continuum starts on the brand you last chose in your assistant, or otherwise your active brand in the app. To change it, say "Switch to Acme Coffee."
</Step>
<Step title="Connect your channels">
> "Connect our Instagram account."

You get a link to approve the connection. Your Instagram needs to be a Business or Creator account; if it's a personal account, switch it in the Instagram app's settings. Do the same for your Meta ads and any other platform you use.
</Step>
<Step title="Ground everything in your brand">
> "Read our brand book and tell me in three lines how we sound."

You get a three-line summary of your voice. From here on, everything the assistant writes is grounded in it.
</Step>
<Step title="See how you're doing">
> "How did we do last week on Instagram and on our Meta ads?"

You get last week's numbers for each connected platform, with the posts and ads that stood out.
</Step>
<Step title="Decide what's next">
> "Based on that, what's the one thing we should do this week?"

You get one recommendation and the reason for it. Nothing is changed until you ask.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Not sure what to ask? Try "What can you help me with in the Calendar?" It's a good way to explore any part of Continuum.</Tip>

## Or hand it one prompt

Paste this into a new chat once the connector is on. Your assistant runs the five steps above and stops to ask you before connecting anything.

<Visibility for="humans">
```text Paste into Claude or ChatGPT
Use the Continuum connector. Tell me which brand I'm on and which platforms are
connected. If Instagram or Meta ads aren't connected, give me the links to connect
them and wait. Then read our brand book and summarize how we sound in three lines,
show me last week's results on Instagram and Meta ads, and finish with the one thing
we should do this week.
```
