The Campaign Flow Canvas is a visual builder for Meta campaigns. Instead of clicking through nested forms, you lay out a campaign as a flow — campaign, ad sets, and ads connected on a canvas — and [Jaina](/scale/jaina) sits alongside to advise while you build.

<Note>This is a different canvas from [AI Studio](/studio/overview). AI Studio makes creative; the Campaign Flow Canvas structures paid campaigns.</Note>

## What you can do

- **Map a campaign visually** — see the campaign → ad set → ad structure as a connected flow.
- **Get advice inline** — a floating Jaina chat answers questions and suggests moves as you work.
