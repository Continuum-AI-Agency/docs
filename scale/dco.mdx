title: "Dynamic creative optimization"
description: "Automated actions that keep your best-performing paid creatives running."
---

Dynamic creative optimization (DCO) is how Continuum keeps your paid creative working without constant manual tuning. It watches performance and surfaces **DCO actions** — automated moves on your creatives, like leaning into winners and pulling back on underperformers.

## Where DCO shows up

- **DCO action alerts** appear on the [paid analytics](/scale/analytics) dashboard and on your Home briefing, so the next move is always in view.
- Actions are grounded in real performance across the ad accounts assigned to your brand.

## Product catalog feeds

For catalog-driven advertising, DCO runs on a **product catalog** — your products, mapped to ad objects with clean tagging so metrics stay accurate. The catalog is managed in Continuum's paid **primitives** and keeps DCO feeds in sync as your catalog changes.
