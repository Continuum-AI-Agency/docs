Jaina is your paid-media analyst. Ask a question in plain language — "why did ROAS drop last week?" — and Jaina analyzes your connected accounts and returns a structured **checkpoint report** rather than a wall of text.

## What a report contains

- **KPI metrics** — the numbers that matter for your question, laid out as a grid.
- **Charts** — visual trends behind the numbers.
- **Strategic insights** — what the data means.
- **Prioritized recommendations** — concrete next steps, each tagged with **impact** and **effort** so you know what to do first.

## Where to use Jaina

- As the **Jaina** tab in [Scale](/scale/overview), with a side canvas and full-screen mode for deeper sessions.
- Embedded in the [Campaign Flow Canvas](/scale/campaign-canvas), advising while you build.

<Tip>Jaina reads the ad accounts assigned to your active brand. Connect and assign accounts in [Integrations](/integrations) first.</Tip>
