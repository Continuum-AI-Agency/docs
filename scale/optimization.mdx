title: "Paid optimization"
description: "Review and apply optimization recommendations across your campaign portfolios."
---

Paid optimization — the Optimizer — surfaces concrete ways to improve paid performance and lets you apply them. It works across **portfolios** of campaigns rather than one campaign at a time.

<Note>The sidebar labels this **Paid Optimization**; inside Scale it's the **Optimization** tab.</Note>

## How it's organized

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The state of optimization across your portfolios at a glance. |
| **Portfolios** | Your campaign portfolios and their pending recommendations. |
| **Actions** | Recommendations to review and apply. |
| **Logs** | A history of what's been actioned. |

## Working with recommendations

The Optimizer generates **pending recommendations** for each portfolio. Review them under **Actions**, apply the ones you want, and track everything that's been done under **Logs**. If a portfolio isn't set up or an account is offline, the Optimizer tells you what to connect first.
