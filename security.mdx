Continuum connects to your ad and social accounts and stores your brand data, so security and privacy matter. This page will grow into a full reference; in the meantime, here's how the essentials work and where to get details.

## Connected accounts

You connect providers through their official OAuth flows and assign only the specific accounts you choose to a brand. You can disconnect a provider or remove an assignment at any time from **Settings → Integrations**.

## Requesting details

For specifics on data handling, retention, subprocessors, and compliance — including documentation for security reviews — contact [support@trycontinuum.ai](mailto:support@trycontinuum.ai).
