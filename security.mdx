Continuum connects to your ad and social accounts and holds your brand's data. Here's how access is controlled, and how you check and revoke it.

## At a glance

| Area | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Assistant sign-in** | OAuth 2.1 with PKCE, using your Continuum login. No API keys. |
| **Access** | Only the brands you belong to, and only what your role allows. |
| **Going live** | Publishing, ad changes, spending, and deletions show a preview and wait for your yes. |
| **Activity** | **Settings → Activity** lists your assistant actions. |
| **Revoking** | **Settings → Connections** lists every connected assistant. Revoke any of them. |
| **Ad and social accounts** | Connect once, assign to a brand. Nothing syncs until you assign it. |

## Signing in your AI assistant

- Claude, ChatGPT, and other assistants connect with **OAuth 2.1**, using the authorization code flow with PKCE.
- There are **no API keys** to create, share, or leak.
- You sign in with your normal Continuum login, pick a brand, and choose **Allow access** or **Deny**.
- Your assistant never sees your Continuum password.

The [server reference](/mcp/reference#authentication) has the protocol details.

## Access follows your role

A connected assistant works as you. It reaches only the brands you belong to and does only what your role on each brand allows. What it can do comes from your brands and roles, not from anything granted at sign-in.

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Viewer** | Read results, plans, and the Library. |
| **Operator** | Also ask Jaina and the Organic agent, and create, change, and run content, creative, and campaigns. |
| **Admin**, **Owner** | Also manage people and settings, approve switching on ads, and produce final versions in Forge. |

See [Roles & permissions](/settings/roles).

## Nothing goes live without a yes

Publishing, ad and budget changes, live creative swaps, final Forge versions, and deletions show a preview and wait for your yes. Each yes works once, applies only to the exact change you previewed, and expires. New campaigns, ad sets, and ads are always created **paused**, and switching one on needs an Admin or Owner. [You stay in control](/mcp/control) has the full list, the one exception, and what happens right away; the [server reference](/mcp/reference#confirmations) has each lifetime.

## See and revoke assistant access

**Settings → Activity** shows what each assistant did as you, and **Settings → Connections** revokes it; [Connections and activity](/settings/connections) has the details. Continuum logs only a fingerprint of each request, never the request itself. If something looks wrong, revoke the connection and contact [support@trycontinuum.ai](mailto:support@trycontinuum.ai).

## Connected ad and social accounts

Integrations follow one rule: **connect once, assign to a brand.**

- You connect a provider through its own OAuth flow.
- You then assign the specific accounts that belong to each brand. Nothing syncs until you assign it, so data stays separate between the brands you manage.
- You can disconnect a provider or remove an assignment at any time from **Settings → Integrations**.

See [Integrations](/integrations).

## Sharing work outside your account

A Library **share link** lets a client or partner see work without an account. You choose whether they can download, comment, or approve, and you can add a passcode. You can also lock a comment so it stays internal. See [Library](/canvas/library).

## What your assistant sees

Claude or ChatGPT sees what you ask and what Continuum sends back to answer it, such as campaign figures, draft captions, and links to images. That conversation follows your assistant's own privacy settings. Continuum's handling of your data is covered in our [privacy policy](https://app.trycontinuum.ai/privacy).

## Requesting details

For data handling, retention, subprocessors, compliance, or documentation for a security review, contact [support@trycontinuum.ai](mailto:support@trycontinuum.ai).

## Try it

- Ask your assistant: "Which brand am I working on, and what's my role on it?" It answers with the brand and your role, so you know what it can do for you.
- To cut off an assistant, go to **Settings → Connections**, find it, and revoke it.
