Billing lives under **Settings → Billing** for the active brand. It's where you see your plan and manage what your brand can do.

## What billing controls

- **Plan** — your subscription, which gates access to areas like [Scale](/scale/overview) and the depth of media analysis in your [Library](/studio/library).
- **AI Studio tier** — your entitlement level for [AI Studio](/studio/overview) generation.
- **Credits** — the balance that generation draws from.

## Managing your plan

Review and change your plan from the billing section. Each brand has its own plan and tier, so you can run brands at different levels.

<Info>For current plans and what each includes, see [pricing](https://trycontinuum.ai/#pricing).</Info>
