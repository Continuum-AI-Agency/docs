Billing lives under **Settings → Billing** for the active brand. Each brand has its own plans and credits, so you can run brands at different levels.

## What's on the billing page

| Section | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Plans** | Your Organic+ and Performance+ plans, which unlock those product lines. A plan whose charge didn't go through shows **Payment failed**. |
| **Canvas credits** | The balance that image, video, voiceover, and music generation draws from, and how much of it has been used this period. See [Providers and rates](/providers-and-rates) for what each generation costs. |
| **Credit packs** | Top up Canvas credits with a one-time pack. Credits you buy never expire. |
| **Auto-billing** | Off unless you turn it on. When the brand runs out of Canvas credits, generation keeps going and usage is billed on your next invoice. Your credits and packs are used first. |
| **Payment method** and **Invoices** | Your card on file and past invoices. |

## When you run out of Canvas credits

Generation pauses for that brand until you buy a credit pack or turn on auto-billing.

<Info>For current plans and what each includes, see [pricing](https://trycontinuum.ai/#pricing).</Info>

## Try it

- **Check your balance before a big batch:** before you ask for "Make ten 8-second clips of our new product", go to **Settings → Billing** and read **Canvas credits**. Compare it with the rates in [Providers and rates](/providers-and-rates).
- **Top up the right brand:** credits land on the active brand. Check that the brand switcher shows the brand you mean, such as "Acme Coffee", then go to **Settings → Billing → Credit packs**, choose a pack, and pay.
- **Keep generating at zero:** go to **Settings → Billing** and turn on **Auto-billing**.

When a brand runs out and auto-billing is off, the node shows "Out of Canvas credits" instead of generating.
