Billing lives under **Settings → Billing** for the active brand. Each brand has its own plans and credits, so you can run brands at different levels.

## What's on the billing page

| Section | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Plans** | Your Organic+ and Performance+ plans, which unlock those product lines. Each plan shows whether it's **Active**, **Activating**, or **Payment failed**. |
| **Canvas credits** | The balance that image, video, voiceover, and music generation draws from, and how much of it has been used this period. See [Providers and rates](/providers-and-rates) for what each generation costs. |
| **Credit packs** | Top up Canvas credits with a one-time pack. Credits are added as soon as payment is confirmed. |
| **Auto-billing** | When the brand runs out of Canvas credits, generation keeps going and usage is billed to your card on the next invoice, up to a monthly cap if one is set. Credit packs are used first. |
| **Payment method** and **Invoices** | Your card on file and past invoices. |

## When you run out of Canvas credits

Generation pauses for that brand until you buy a credit pack or turn on auto-billing.
