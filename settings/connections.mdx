---

When you connect Claude, ChatGPT, or another assistant to Continuum, two settings pages keep it accountable: one lists the connections, the other lists what they did.

## Connections

**Settings → Connections** lists every assistant you've connected to Continuum. Revoke any you no longer use. To use one again later, [connect it](/mcp/connect) and sign in once more.

<Note>These are AI assistant connections. Your social and ad platforms are managed separately under **Settings → Integrations**. See [Integrations](/integrations).</Note>

## Activity

**Settings → Activity** lists every action you've taken through a connected assistant, and when. Each person sees their own activity.

Use it to check what an assistant did on your behalf. If something looks wrong, revoke the connection under **Settings → Connections** and contact [support@trycontinuum.ai](mailto:support@trycontinuum.ai).
