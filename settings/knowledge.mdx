The knowledge base is where you give Continuum source material about your brand and business. The documents you add here feed your [brand book](/brand/overview), the [agents](/concepts), and [Jaina](/performance/jaina), so their output reflects what's actually true about you.

## What to add

Upload the documents that describe your brand and strategy — brand guidelines, positioning docs, product information, past campaigns, and research. The more grounded material Continuum has, the stronger your [brand readiness](/brand/readiness) and the sharper every generation.

## Strategic analysis

From the knowledge base you can **run a strategic analysis** that synthesizes your documents into a consolidated brand guideline. This gives the agents a single, coherent reference instead of a pile of raw files.
