Continuum settings are split into two scopes, and each teammate has a role that sets what they can do.

## Brand scope vs. account scope

- **Brand scope** — settings for the active brand: **Activation**, **General** (identity and members), **Brand Intelligence**, **Skills**, **Prompts**, **Integrations**, **Knowledge**, and **Billing**.
- **Account scope** — settings for you as a user: **Profile**, **Connections** (your connected AI assistants), **Activity**, **Brands**, **Notifications**, and **Drive**.

## Roles

Each member of a brand has one of these roles:

| Role | Broadly can |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything an Admin can, as the brand's owner. |
| **Admin** | Manage the brand end to end, including members and billing. |
| **Operator** | Do the day-to-day work — create, generate, and publish. |
| **Viewer** | View the brand's work without making changes. |

<Info>The exact capabilities for each role are shown in the in-app role legend under **Settings → General**.</Info>

## Roles in your AI assistant

A connected assistant works as you, so your role on each brand applies there too.

| Role | From your assistant, can |
| --- | --- |
| **Viewer** | Read results, the content plan, the Library, the brand book, budget recommendations, pipelines, and Forge templates. |
| **Operator** | Also ask Jaina and the Organic agent; draft, approve, and publish posts; generate on the canvas and run pipelines; edit video; comment on and review Library work; save brand material; create and change Meta ads; approve or reject budget recommendations; and fill Forge templates. |
| **Admin**, **Owner** | Also approve switching on a campaign, ad set, or ad, or a large budget increase, with `mcp_approval_grant`; and produce final versions in [Forge](/forge/overview). |

Some brands require an Admin or Owner to approve every publish and paid change. On those brands, an Operator's yes waits for that approval. See [You stay in control](/mcp/control).

## Managing your team

Invite teammates and set their roles from **Settings → General**, or during [onboarding](/quickstart). You can change a member's role or remove them later from the same place.

## Try it

- **Add a reviewer:** to let a freelancer see work without changing anything, go to **Settings → General**, invite them by email, such as "freelancer@example.com", and choose **Viewer**.
- **Check your own role:** ask your assistant "What's my role on this brand?"
- **Approve a change as an Admin:** ask your assistant "Show me the paid changes waiting for my approval."
