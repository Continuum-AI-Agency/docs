The asset library is your centralized hub for all creative files. Every image, video, logo, font, and design element your team uses lives here — organized, searchable, and always accessible.

## Uploading assets

Upload files by dragging them into the asset library or clicking **+ Upload**. Supported formats:

| Type | Formats |
| --- | --- |
| **Images** | PNG, JPG, WEBP, SVG |
| **Video** | MP4, MOV, WEBM |
| **Documents** | PDF |
| **Fonts** | TTF, OTF, WOFF, WOFF2 |

<Info>There are no file count limits. Storage limits depend on your plan — see [pricing](https://trycontinuum.ai/#pricing) for details.</Info>

## Organizing assets

Keep your library structured with:

- **Folders** — group assets by campaign, brand, channel, or any custom hierarchy.
- **Tags** — add searchable labels to assets for fast retrieval.
- **Filters** — filter by type, upload date, uploader, or tag.
- **Search** — full-text search across file names and tags.

## Brand assets

Brand assets are a special category of files that are tied to your brand profile:

- **Logos** — primary, secondary, and icon versions.
- **Color palettes** — your approved brand colors, available everywhere in the editor.
- **Fonts** — your brand typefaces, automatically applied in templates and the Canvas.
- **Style guides** — uploaded reference PDFs or images for your team.

Brand assets can be **locked** so they cannot be deleted or modified without admin approval.

## Using assets in creatives

Assets are accessible throughout Continuum:

- **Canvas Studio** — drag assets from the library directly onto the canvas.
- **Templates** — set asset slots that pull from specific folders or tags.
- **Campaigns** — attach assets to campaign briefs for team reference.
- **AI generation** — reference existing assets when generating new variations.

<Tip>Tag your best-performing assets with "top performer" so your team can quickly find proven creative elements.</Tip>
