The Library is your brand's media home. It unifies everything visual — files you upload, creative you generate in AI Studio, canvas outputs, clips, reels, and re-hosted competitor creative — into one searchable, semantically indexed grid.

## Media and Inspiration

The Library has two views:

- **Media** — your brand's own images and video.
- **Inspiration** — competitor organic posts and paid ad creatives to draw from. This is the same engine as [Brand Spy](/brand/spy), embedded here.

## Where media comes from

Every asset carries its **source**, so you can browse by where it came from:

| Source | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Upload** | A file you added. |
| **AI generated** | Output from an AI Studio generation. |
| **Canvas** | An artifact produced on the canvas. |
| **Clip** / **Reel** | A generated short clip or a stitched reel. |
| **Hyperframe** | A composited motion graphic. |
| **Inspiration** | A re-hosted competitor creative. |
| **Chat upload** / **Backfill** | Media added through chat or imported in bulk. |

## Finding and organizing

- **Semantic search** finds media by what's in it, not just the file name.
- **Detected-object overlays** highlight what an image contains; toggle them on or off.
- **Collections** group related media for a campaign or theme.

<Info>Deeper analysis of your media depends on your plan. See [pricing](https://trycontinuum.ai/#pricing).</Info>

## Using media

Media flows into the rest of Continuum:

- **Drag it onto the [canvas](/studio/canvas)** as a reference.
- **Generate clips** from longer video.
- **Publish** finished video to the [content calendar](/organic/calendar).
- **Save competitor inspiration** to boards from the Inspiration view.
