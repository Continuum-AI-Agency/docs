AI Studio is where you make creative. It's an infinite **canvas** where you add **nodes** — prompts, references, and generators — and connect them into a **workflow** that produces images and video. Everything you make is grounded in your brand book and lands in your [Library](/studio/library).

<Note>In the sidebar this area is labeled **Canvas**. The workspace header reads **AI Studio** — they're the same place.</Note>

## What's on the canvas

- A **node library** on the left to add image, video, and utility nodes.
- The **canvas** itself, where you arrange and connect nodes.
- A **media library** panel on the right to drag in references and reuse past outputs.

## What you can do

- **Generate images and video** with selectable models (Nano Banana, Veo, Kling) — see [the canvas](/studio/canvas).
- **Edit and sequence video** in-browser with the splicer and timeline editor.
- **Save reusable workflows** and apply **creative skills** — see [Workflows & skills](/studio/workflows).
- **Ground every generation in your brand book** so output stays on-brand by default.
- **Publish straight to the organic calendar** with a Publish to Planner node.
- **Collaborate in real time** — teammates' cursors and selections appear live on the canvas.
